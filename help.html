Help centre

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, tap Checkout, log in or sign up, enter your delivery address and place the order.

How can I pay?

Cash on delivery is available. You pay when the order arrives.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free on orders of ₹499 and above after discounts. Otherwise it is ₹49.

Where is my order?

Open My orders to see its current status.

Returns and cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and we will help. See the Contact page.

I forgot my password

On the login page, type your email and tap "Forgot password?" to get a reset link.
